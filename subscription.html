How this maps to a box-subscription brand

  • Same backwards math: first-box AOV + reorder curve replace trial → rebill
  • Allowed CAC per offer type; bidding favors subscriber starts over one-time orders
  • Cohort dashboard by acquisition week: reorder retention, churn after shipment N, payback date
  • Weekly budget reallocation by cohort payback — never by front-end ROAS alone
  • Honest note: I haven't run a physical-box subscription yet — the LTV/cohort machinery transfers one-to-one; box-specific churn patterns and offer structures are what I'd ramp on fast
