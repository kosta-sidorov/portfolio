How I run acquisition

Subscription apps punish buyers who optimize what the platform shows them. These principles repeat across every account I have owned.

01

Bid to LTV, not to installs

Target CPA is derived from lifetime value per GEO, not set as one flat number. A $2 install in a market that never rebills is more expensive than a $6 install in one that does.

02

The event ladder is the product

Install → trial → paywall → purchase, wired correctly and identically across every channel. Most scaling problems in app UA are measurement problems wearing a costume.

03

Attribution I can defend

Singular, AppsFlyer, Firebase, SKAN — whatever the stack, the standard is the same: I can explain where a number comes from and where it stops being reliable. View-through never drives a decision.

04

Read the funnel past the click

Rebill rate, decline rate, refunds. The most expensive problem I ever found was invisible in every ad metric and only appeared two steps after the conversion the platform reports.

05

Creative is a system, not a task

Hook-first concepts briefed against install-to-trial and trial-to-paid, iterated in volume with the creative team. CTR is a symptom, not a goal.
